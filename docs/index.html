Why Fader

macOS only gives you one volume knob for everything. Fader gives every app its own.

01 · Per-app control

Turn down the app. Not everything.

Every app making noise gets its own slider. Drag Spotify down without touching your Zoom call, and stop choosing between the two.

02 · Auto-lower for calls

Never scramble for the pause button.

The second Zoom, Teams, or Slack picks up, everything else quiets down on its own, then comes right back when the call ends.

03 · Instant mute

Silence one app. Instantly.

Tap the speaker icon and that one app goes quiet. Everything else keeps playing exactly as it was.

Under 1% CPU No mic access, ever Menu bar only, no Dock icon Works with your volume keys No account needed

Fader.dmg is downloading

Quick heads up: Fader isn't on Apple's paid developer program yet, so macOS blocks it the first time you open it. Takes about 10 seconds to clear, and only happens once:

  1. Open Fader.dmg and drag Fader into Applications, then open it.
  2. macOS says it's blocked (that's expected, not an error).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway (confirm your password if it asks).

You can check exactly what Fader does before running it, or see Apple's own instructions for this step.
