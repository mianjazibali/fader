FAQ

Does it trigger the macOS recording indicator?

Yes, the purple one — that's the system-audio indicator, and it's expected for any app that adjusts other apps' sound, same as SoundSource or Audio Hijack. It never triggers the orange microphone dot, since Fader never touches your mic.

Why isn't this just built into macOS?

Apple ships per-app volume on iOS but not on the Mac. Fader builds the UI Apple didn't.

Does it work with Bluetooth headphones / AirPods?

Yes — plug in, unplug, or switch outputs anytime. Fader follows automatically, no restart needed.

Will it slow down my Mac?

No — well under 1% CPU, even while it's actively adjusting audio. Idle, it uses effectively nothing.

How is this different from SoundSource?

SoundSource is a polished commercial app ($39) with EQ and routing. Fader is free and open source, focused on the core "turn one app down" use case.

Where's the code?

It lives at github.com/mianjazibali/fader. MIT licensed — read it, fork it, ship PRs.
