Why Fader

macOS only gives you one volume knob for everything. Fader gives every app its own.

01
Per-app sliders
Every app that's making noise gets its own slider. Drag to turn it up or down, instantly.
02
One-click mute
Tap the speaker icon to silence any one app. Everything else keeps playing.
03
Quiets down for calls
The moment Zoom, Teams, or Slack picks up, everything else automatically turns down — no fumbling for the pause button.
04
Plays nice with your volume keys
Every app's slider is relative to your Mac's system volume, so your F11/F12 keys still work exactly like they always have.
05
You won't notice it's running
Well under 1% CPU, even while actively mixing audio. No fans spinning up, no battery drain.
06
Menu bar only
No Dock icon, no window on launch. Just a small icon in your menu bar, there when you need it.
07
No mic access, ever
Fader never touches your microphone. You'll see macOS's purple recording dot while it runs — that's expected for any app that adjusts audio, same as SoundSource.
08
Open source
Free, MIT licensed, and fully inspectable on GitHub. File an issue, ship a PR.
